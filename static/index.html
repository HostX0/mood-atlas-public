        A LITTLE HELP

        Quick start

        1. Import tracks. Upload Excel or CSV files, or try the sample.
        2. Match your songs. Use automatic matching and review exceptions.
        3. Transfer ready tracks. Confirm the YouTube Music account and playlist.
        What if my connection drops?

        Temporary failures retry automatically. Open Mood Atlas again to continue a saved job. If YouTube needs you to sign in or switch accounts, the app shows the next step. Keep Chrome and YouTube Music open during transfers.

        Can I review multiple songs together?

        Use automatic matching, or select several tracks and approve suitable suggestions together. Check artist and recording differences in review.

        How do I change a choice?

        Use Undo after approving or deferring, or change the match before transferring. Transferred tracks are available to view.

        What if the recording is missing?

        Search again, paste the recording link, or defer the track and move the rest.

        How do I move to another browser?

        Download a backup from Library options and restore it in the other browser. Connect YouTube Music there to continue.

        What happens to my data?

        Files and progress are stored in this browser. Searches send song titles and artist names to the Mood Atlas server, which queries YouTube Music. The extension uses the YouTube Music session in your browser to write playlists.

        How does matching work?

        Mood Atlas compares the title, artist, and recording details. You can review and change suggestions before transferring. YouTube Music can slow down or reject requests.
